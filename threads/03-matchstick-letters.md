## [SPECULATION]

- **Group these letters by how tightly they're tied to the mystery ([H12], user 2026-06-13).** The **matchstick** letters
  here — `EC`, `J+M`, `S+J` — are **Group 2**: tied by **co-location and props**, not by the carved-geometry technique that
  binds the Butcher Creek `LJ`/`SM` (**Group 1**, the firmer anchor). Two of the matchstick sets get pulled out of "just
  flavour," though: **`EC`** sits beside the **Black Widow spider card**, so it joins `LJ`/`SM` as the **hybrid** spider-flagged
  subset; and **`J+M`** is singled out because its **location *is* a confirmed node** — Cornwall Kerosene & Tar, the
  spider-engraving START pole. So treat `J+M` as load-bearing through *where* it sits even if the matchsticks are background.
  Full framework + the test rationale in [connections §1 (Letter GROUPS)](../analysis/connections.md).
- **The letters across all clues:** carved `LJ`, `SM` (Butcher Creek #4) + matches `EC` (Vetter's Echo), `J+M` (Cornwall K&T),
  `S+J` (Caliga Hall). Multiset **{C, E, J, J, J, L, M, M, S, S}** (J×3, M×2, S×2; C, E, L once).
  - **J is the hub:** in `LJ`, `J+M`, `S+J`. **S** in `SM`, `S+J`. **M** in `SM`, `J+M`.
  - The matches use a connective **`+`** ("J + M", "S + J") — **lovers'-initials** styling — vs. the bare carved pairs
    (`LJ`, `SM`). Read as **people/relationships** (who pairs with whom) or **chain** by shared letter: `L–J`, `J–M`, `S–J`,
    `S–M`, `E–C`. Full graph in [connections](../analysis/connections.md).
- **Or the matchsticks are NUMBERS, not people ([S21], user 2026-06-13).** Same letters, different read: map A1Z26 and let the
  **displayed punctuation pick the operator** — a **`+` pair adds**, a **bare pair concatenates**. So `J+M` = 10+13 = **23**,
  `S+J` = 19+10 = **29**, and `EC` (bare, no `+`) = 5,3 = **"53"** — i.e. the matchsticks as **three numbers {53, 23, 29}** or
  **one number "532329"**. 🟢 All three are **prime** (cf. the Window Rock mural's prime counts 2,3,5,7); 🟢 **`EC` = 53 = the
  5-black/3-red feather split** ([S18], and `EC` sits by the Black Widow card). 🔴 But the set matches **no** known number line
  (Gertrude's `1237645112`, the 1–7 tallies, "five poles west"), and the operator rule is chosen to fit — so **weak,
  coincidence-prone**, a live candidate not a finding. Arithmetic verified in [`number_grid.py`](../experiments/number_grid.py);
  detail in [connections §1a](../analysis/connections.md).
- **Location is itself a layer.** Three of the four letter-sites are *already* mystery nodes — Butcher Creek (trail start),
  **Cornwall K&T (web-trail start; holds `J+M`)**, Vetter's Echo (Black Widow card). The fourth, **Caliga Hall**, is the
  **Gray** estate — rivals of the **Braithwaites**, i.e. Gertrude's family ([thread 04](04-gertrude-numbers.md)). So the
  matchsticks may pair **letters AND places** (e.g. does `J+M` "belong" to Cornwall, `S+J` to the Grays?).
- **`EC` + Black Widow + letters to Annabella — letters now READ ([U16] resolved-negative, 2026-06-13).** The desk holds two
  poems (the **"Dear Annabella Poem"** + the unsigned **"A Day's Walk Poem"**). Recipient is **"Annabella"** (first name only);
  the poem is **unsigned** — no author name/initials. Authorship is *implied* **Vetter / P.H.V.** (location lore, real hermit
  Phillip Henry Vetter), **not** `E.C.` So the correspondence does **not** name the `EC` initials — `EC` is purely the
  matchsticks. The `E–C` pair stays the isolated graph component with no name behind it (yet). *(Red Dead Wiki, B-tier.)*
- **On the "dev initials" counter-hypothesis — weighted DOWN (your argument):** RDR2 had **thousands** of credited workers, so
  for almost *any* two-letter pair you could find *some* matching staffer — meaning a coincidental match is **near-certain and
  therefore weak evidence**. Low-level staff are very unlikely to get initials hidden in-world; only **founders / senior
  creative leads** (e.g. Sam & Dan Houser, key directors) would plausibly be honoured this way, and even then not as a *set of
  matchstick puzzles next to a Black Widow card at four themed locations*. **Working stance:** treat the matchstick letters as
  an **in-fiction mystery** (related to the spider puzzle or a parallel one); only bother checking dev initials against the
  **short list of high-ranking/notable people**, and even a hit there is suggestive, not proof.
- **⚠️ "Initials" is itself a hypothesis ([U25], user 2026-06-13).** The **[KNOWN]** is only that these are **letters** displayed
  as bare pairs (`LJ`,`SM`) and `+`-pairs (`J+M`,`S+J`,`EC`). Whether they're **initials of names** is [SPECULATION]. Two
  **non-name** readings were tested (see [connections §1a](../analysis/connections.md)):
  - **Anagram/whole-set cipher — NEGATIVE** ([`letters_cipher.py`](../experiments/letters_cipher.py)): the set
    `{C,E,J,J,J,L,M,M,S,S}` is vowel-starved (1 vowel) + J-heavy → can't form words; meant to be read as **pairs**. A
    positional per-pair cipher stays open but needs an ordering key. One flag → **[S18]**: `EC` in A1Z26 = **5, 3** = the
    5-black/3-red feather split, `EC` being the marking by the Black Widow card (weak/coincidence-prone).
  - **Place-abbreviation — mostly NEGATIVE → [S19]**: 🟢 `EC` = **Emerald Crossing**, 🟢 `SM` = **Scarlett Meadows** (holds
    Braithwaite Manor + Caliga Hall) are exact place matches; 🔴 but `LJ`/`J+M`/`S+J` have **no `J`-named place** (RDR2 has
    almost none), so the location reading fails for most. No community source proposed it.
