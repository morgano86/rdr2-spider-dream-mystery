# Thread 07 - The Van der Linde gang roster (fates · death timing · graves) + the initials cross-check

> *New here? See the [README](../README.md) for the overview and the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the ID/tag conventions (`K13`, `[#89]`, `H27`…).*

**Why this thread is in a spider-mystery repo.** [analysis/connections.md](../analysis/connections.md) has long carried an open task: *"build the RDR2 character/credits name list and test the initials `L.J.`/`S.M.`/`J.M.`/`S.J.`/`E.C.`"* against the mystery's carved/placed letters ([U4]/[U5]). The Van der Linde gang **is** that in-fiction name list - the most prominent set of named people in the game. So this roster is both (a) a standalone reference the investigator asked for, and (b) the candidate-name source for the **letter-markings** puzzle. *(Note [U25]: that the letters are **initials of names** at all is a [SPECULATION] reading - this thread tests that hypothesis, it doesn't presume it.)* The headline result is in the [cross-check](#the-letter--name-cross-check-speculation) below: **two markings map to major gang members on the nose** - **`SM` → Sean MacGuire** and **`J+M` → John Marston** - and the gang supplies the **`L`** that Register Rock could not. The one marking that matches **nothing, anywhere** is **`EC`**.

> **Source tier for the whole roster:** **[KNOWN]**, Red Dead Wiki (B-tier, via MediaWiki API, 2026-06-13), with the underlying events verifiable in-game (A-tier). Death timing (chapter + mission name) is the key detail and was cross-checked against the dedicated mission pages; the one wiki extraction error we caught is flagged under [caveats](#caveats--unknowns). Two researcher passes (roster/fates/timing and graves) were reconciled into the tables below.

### Arthur's grave (honor-dependent) ⭐
- **Location (does NOT change by honor):** **Grizzlies East, Ambarino** - a hilltop SE of the **Mysterious Hill Home**, **NE of Bacchus Station/Bridge**. Charles buried him facing the **sunset (west)**. *(Note the proximity to **[Bacchus Bridge](../locations/bacchus-bridge.md)** - the [K22] empty-heart / bird-carving node. Same Grizzlies-East pocket of the map; flagged for the spider repo, not asserted as connected.)*
- **What DOES change by honor at death** *(this is the real honor-dependent detail - the location/orientation does **not** move; an earlier framing that the grave "faces differently by honor" is **not supported** by any source)*:
  - **High honor:** pristine headstone with flowers; an **eagle** appears. Epitaph: *"Blessed are those who hunger and thirst for righteousness."* (Matthew 5:6)
  - **Low honor:** weathered headstone, no flowers; a **coyote** appears. Epitaph: *"Blessed are those who mourn, for they will be comforted."* (Matthew 5:4)
  - The **death scene** itself also varies by honor **and** by the player choice to *help John escape* vs *go back for the money* (peaceful sunrise death vs Micah executing Arthur).

## The letter / name cross-check ([SPECULATION])

Run [`../experiments/name_match.py`](../experiments/name_match.py) (which now carries this roster). Against the mystery markings `LJ` · `SM` · `EC` · `J+M` · `S+J` (multiset `{C,E,J,J,J,L,M,M,S,S}`):

- **🟢 `SM` → Sean MacGuire** - an **exact** one-person initial hit. Sean is a named gang member who **dies in the Rhodes ambush** (and whose killers are the **Gray family** - the same Grays behind **Caliga Hall**, the `S+J` matchstick site).
- **🟢 `J+M` → John Marston** (and Jack Marston) - an **exact** hit, and `J+M` uses the **lovers'-`+`** styling; John is the central Marston.
- **🟢 The gang supplies the `L`** that **Register Rock entirely lacked** - **Lenny Summers** / **Leopold Strauss** - so a re-paired reading of `LJ` (e.g. **L**enny + **J**ohn) is now at least *populated*, where the rock left the `L` leg empty.
- **🔴 `EC` matches nothing** - **no single `E.C.` gang member, and no gang member with an `E` first name** (the only `E` is Javier **E**scuella's surname). `EC` also had no referent in the **Annabella letters** ([U16], resolved-negative) and no one-person hit on **Register Rock**. **`EC` is the odd one out across every name source we have.**

> **[S16]** - *The carved/matchstick letters may encode **Van der Linde gang members' initials**.* Anchored by the two exact hits (Sean MacGuire `SM`, John Marston `J+M`) and the newly-populated `L` leg. **Caution (per the [S13]/dev-initials reasoning):** with ~27 members, *some* initial overlap is expected by chance, so the bare existence of matches is weak; the *signal* is that the two exact one-person hits are **major, plot-central characters** (not obscure ones) and that they land on markings already tied to mystery nodes. **[SPECULATION], not proof.** ⚠️ **The coincidence-odds calc is now DONE** ([`../experiments/initials_likelihood.py`](../experiments/initials_likelihood.py)) and it **softens S16**: 2-of-5 hits is P≈0.04 under uniform letters but **≈0.59** under a realistic name-initial-frequency null (the markings use the commonest initials J/S/M). So the *count* is weak; S16's only weight is the unmodellable "major characters" point. Suggestive, not evidence. *(Also tested & rejected as whole-set readings: anagram, place-names [S19], numeric/grid [U26].)*

> **[S17]** - *`EC`'s persistent non-match is itself evidence the initials are a deliberate puzzle (investigator's point, 2026-06-13).* Three of the four other markings sit at mystery nodes and two map to major characters, yet **`EC` - the one placed literally beside the Black Widow spider cigarette card, the most on-theme object of the lot - resists every name source** (unsigned Annabella poems, no Register Rock person, no gang member). An ordinary background detail would be expected to be as mundane as the rest, not to be the **spider-flagged** one that no in-fiction name explains. That asymmetry raises the prior that the letters were **chosen, not incidental**. ⚠️ **Honest caveat:** this is an **argument from absence** - "we can't find the referent" is not the same as "a hidden referent exists" (the wiki still records the matches' meaning as *unknown*). It strengthens the *case for deliberateness*; it does not decode anything. Tagged weak-but-recorded.

## Caveats & [UNKNOWN]s

- **Arthur's death mission - wiki extraction error caught.** The raw *Arthur Morgan* wikitext returned the mission as *"A Fork in the Road"* - **wrong** (that is a different Ch.6 mission). The dedicated mission page confirms Arthur dies in the final mission ***"Red Dead Redemption,"*** after ***"Our Best Selves."*** Treat any "A Fork in the Road" attribution as a bad read.
- **"Paying Respects" exact membership** - the achievement surfaces the companion graves, but the precise count and whether **Arthur's own grave** (and **Eagle Flies**, a Wapiti tribe member, not gang) are counted in the set is **not nailed down here** - left [UNKNOWN] rather than asserted. The individual grave *locations* above are each separately sourced.
- **Off-screen / ambiguous deaths:** Mac Callander, Leopold Strauss (both reported, not shown), and **Karen Jones** (only Tilly's speculation) - recorded as stated, not over-claimed.
- **Open long-term fates (alive at RDR2's end):** Charles, Sadie, Trelawny, Swanson - deliberately left open by the game.
- **The real open question for *this* repo ([U4]/[U5]):** does the gang roster actually *feed* the initials puzzle, or are the Sean/John hits coincidence? Needs the coincidence-odds script + any in-fiction link tying a gang name to a marking's *location* (the Sean ↔ Gray-family ↔ Caliga Hall thread is the most suggestive so far).
