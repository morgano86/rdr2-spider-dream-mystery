> The '''Spider Dream Mystery''' is an Easter egg and unsolved mystery in *Red Dead Redemption 2* and *Red Dead Online*.

## Part 1
The hunt begins at **Butcher Creek**, where the Butcher Creek pentagram can be found glowing between **4–5 am**. Inspecting the nearby outhouses, players will find **tally marks** etched inside them that, when connected in order, form a **pentagram** shape. The **fourth outhouse** has extra symbols on it including an **"LJ"**, **"SM"**, and a symbol **depicting Fort Brennand**. The outhouse in **Fort Brennand** has **six** tally marks and on one of the towers inside Fort Brennand there are **seven** tally marks on the entrance. Inside that tower are **three more symbols** depicting a **telephone pole**, a **factory**, and an **oil puddle**.

*Gallery:* Butcher Creek Pentagram (start point) · Outhouse tally numbers forming a pentagram · 4th outhouse: Fort Brennand symbol + "LJ" and "SM" · Fort Brennand tally marks + telephone-pole/factory/oil-puddle symbols.

## Part 2
The symbols on Fort Brennand direct the player to a **telephone pole near Cornwall Kerosene & Tar**, which features a **spider carving** on it. Atop this pole is a **web with a feather** that can **only** be seen at a very specific time of day. The spider engraving shows the **direction of 7 other webs** following the same pattern. These webs show up at night (2–3am, 3–4am, etc.) and will not appear otherwise. At the **center** of the spider is another set of webs that show up between **1–2am** and have **no feathers**. These webs line up to form an **"N"** and a **telephone pole**.

*Gallery:* Spider engraving on the pole by Cornwall Kerosene & Tar · Engraving shows other web locations; the "eye" directs to more poles · A web near **Saint Denis** that only appears 3–4am · Center webs lined up to reveal the **N** + telephone pole.

## Part 3
Traveling **directly north** of the center webs leads to a telephone pole that can be **shot into to reveal an inscription** reading **"W ✞✞✞✞✞"**. The 'crosses' are actually **telephone poles**, and W = **West**. Travel **5 poles west** to find another pole; shoot it many times to find another inscription reading **"NW"** with a symbol that **looks like a guitar**.

*Gallery:* Pole inscription pointing toward the guitar pole · The guitar pole (revealed after many shots) · NW of the guitar pole may lead to Fort Wallace, where two guitars can be found.

## Part 4: A web of ideas  *(wiki marks this section SPECULATIVE)*
Everything past the guitar symbol is heavily debated. Some think it isn't a guitar but a shape in the rocks/map to travel **northwest** of. Others focus on **Fort Wallace** (two guitars inside, pointing different directions). Some think the guitar is a **red herring** and that **shooting the feathers in a certain pattern/condition** triggers something hidden.

**Directional Theory:** following the guitars' heading lands people at **Dodd's Bluff**, **Vetter's Echo**, and **Window Rock**.
- **Window Rock — Strange Statues mural:** depicts birds with differing amounts of black & red feathers plus many symbols; two birds spawn atop Window Rock and sun through the center *could* lead somewhere — inconclusive.
- **Vetter's Echo — the matches:** matches laid on the desk form the letters **"EC"**, right next to the **Black Widow spider cigarette card**. In the desk underneath are the **letters to Annabella** and a **cheat code** (higher wanted level). The matches' meaning is unknown, but **three other sets of matches** like this exist around the map depicting **"J + M"**, **"S + J"**, and an **arrow pointing toward a stash**.

**Native Connection:** feathers + spider may root the solution in Native American history/storyline; cf. the **Spider Grandmother** figure (gave souls, separated tribes, serves a cursory role often as a spider).

**Birds Theory:** in Fort Wallace's westernmost tower (one guitar) the roof has two **"w"/bird** symbols made from the tower's geometry and moss texture. Could be a modeling error or **developer initials** — but the *same geometry-hidden-symbol method* is used for the Butcher Creek outhouses and **nowhere else**, so it's debated. Strange Man (YouTube) argues the birds indicate the nearby **flock leading to the Giant's cave**, and that a **question-mark shape** is visible from there outside the map — others call this **pareidolia**. Some read the symbols as exactly **two birds** (searched Two Crows etc., inconclusive).

## Additional Notes
- The web feathers are called **"spiderdream"** in the files; they respawn when shot; texture **"wap_gen_feather01"**. Total **five black feathers and three red feathers** in the webs found.
- May be **cut content**: NW of the guitar pole points toward a **beta location** (believed Wapiti, an army camp, or Iron Cloud) and toward **Fort Wallace, Chez Porter, Cotorra Springs, Tempest Rim, Bacchus Bridge**, and **directly to the very tip of Spider Gorge**. **Spider Gorge** is notable — has "spider" in the name and a **vaguely guitar-shaped section**; Fort Wallace has a guitar inside.
- Similar to the **dreamcatchers** side mission, which oddly **does not properly disappear from the log** after completion.
- **GTA V's Mount Chiliad** also has **two webs made of cables** that only spawn between **1–2am**.
- Referenced in a **Nazar Speaks** arcade-machine fortune (GTA Online, Diamond Casino Heist, 2019): *"I see a web, still tangled after years of unraveling. Will you be the one? I wonder…"*
