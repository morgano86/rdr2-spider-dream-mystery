## Thematically resonant phrases — [SPECULATION]
Ranked by fit to the mystery's motifs (dreams, sight/searching, fate, death, nature):
- **KEEP YOUR DREAMS LIGHT / SIMPLE** — *Dreams.* On-the-nose for a "Spider **Dream**" mystery; LIGHT is on our trail (Oil Fields).
- **YOU LONG FOR SIGHT AND SEE NOTHING** — *seeing nothing.* Eerily apt for symbols only visible at certain hours / easily dismissed as nothing; echoes the GTA **Nazar** fortune *"I see a web, still tangled…"*.
- **I SEEK AND I FIND / I STILL SEEK MORE / BE GREEDY ONLY FOR FORESIGHT** — *searching / foresight* (the hunt itself).
- **YOU FLOURISH BEFORE YOU DIE / A SIMPLE LIFE, A BEAUTIFUL DEATH / DEATH IS SILENCE** — *death/fate* (Butcher Creek's folk-horror register).
- **BALANCE. ALL IS BALANCE / THE LUCKY BE STRONG EVERMORE** — *fate/cosmic balance* (loosely, the Spider-Grandmother "weaver of fate" reading).

**Honest caveat:** **no cheat phrase mentions spiders, webs, or feathers**, and **no source links any cheat to the solution.** This is tonal overlap, not a cipher. Keep it as colour, not evidence.
