# One puzzle or two? — adjudicating [U3] (2026-07-04)

**The question ([U3], open since the corpus began):** are the 2018-era carving clues (Butcher Creek → Fort Brennand →
the Heartlands) and the 2025-decoded telegraph-pole web trail the **same puzzle**, **sequential stages**, or **parallel
Easter eggs sharing motifs**? It has sat at rank 2 on the dashboard and in the open-analysis-tasks list for weeks while
evidence accumulated around it ([S22]/[H21], [S28], [S31], [K39]/[S29], [H27], the [#52] datamine). This file is the
desk adjudication — a weighing of what the corpus already holds, **no new evidence**. The verdict is minted as
**[S41]** (§5).

**Why U3 stalled:** it bundles **three separable questions**, and they have different answers:

- **(a) The relay question** — is the 2018 carving chain one continuous designed relay with the 2025 pole/web trail?
  → **Decided toward ONE** (§2).
- **(b) The mechanic question** — is the web *feather/boundary system* a second interactive puzzle grafted onto that
  relay, or part of it / epiphenomenal? → **Genuinely open, and already tracked elsewhere** ([U29]) (§3).
- **(c) The satellite question** — which side-threads (Gertrude, the matchsticks, Bacchus…) belong to the same
  construction? → **Per-thread scorecard**, mostly undecided (§4).

Treating these as one yes/no is what kept U3 unanswerable. Separated, most of it is no longer open.

### The strongest leg: Link 2 is retro-validated, not just asserted

Link 2 is the seam U3 lives on, and it does **not** ride on symbol-reading alone. The pole the symbols were read as
naming was checked, and it turned out to carry (i) a hidden **spider engraving** and (ii) a **time-gated web + feather**
standing at the head of an eight-web, hour-laddered, boundary-scripted system that no one had documented in seven years
([K11], [K13a], [K21]/[K31]). A *misread* pointer does not land, by luck, on the entrance to the game's most elaborate
hidden system. Either the symbols point there by design, or we accept a coincidence far larger than any the corpus
polices elsewhere. (This is the same validate-by-what-you-find logic as the [K15] carving test.)

### Cross-cutting ties (design-level, each independent of the links)

- **Shared signature.** Both halves use the same concealment grammar ([H13]): carvings in wood / content on telegraph
  poles, time-gating, near-invisibility — and both sat undiscovered ~7 years ([K2]).
- **Both are base-game (Oct 2018).** The webs are original-to-launch, not patched in ([K24] pass) — which kills the
  most natural "two puzzles" story, a later-added separate egg.
- **A shared physical node.** Heartland Oil Fields hosts a 2018-era carved clue ([K10] *"KEEP YOUR DREAMS LIGHT"*)
  **and** web `B56`. The two halves overlap at a site, not just in style.
- **The dream motif closes a loop.** The web feathers' internal asset name is **`spiderdream`** ([K13]); the only
  carved in-world text about dreams sits at that shared node ([K10]); and the community's name for the whole mystery —
  on *both* sides — is the Spider **Dream**. The asset layer and the carved layer use the same word.

### Counters, weighed honestly

1. **Community-assembly risk.** The chain as narrated is a late-2025 community assembly; links could be correlated
   over-reading by one interpretive wave. *Mitigation:* the trail half was multiply corroborated by independent outlets
   ([#60]); Link 2 is asserted by the primary authority; and the retro-validation leg is assembly-independent — the
   engraving and the web system exist regardless of who narrated the pointer.
2. **[H11] (third symbol = Register Rock).** If true, the oil-pool leg of Link 2 weakens — but the **pole + factory**
   symbols still name Cornwall K&T (a kerosene/tar works with the engraved pole beside it), and the retro-validation
   stands. [H11] contests one symbol's referent, not the destination.
3. **Motifs travel between unrelated eggs** — the corpus's own lesson ([S33]/[U34]: Mount Shann ↔ Chiliad). *Scoped:*
   Mount Shann shares only motifs. The web trail shares an **addressed pointer**, a **physical node**, and the
   **signature medium** — a different class of tie.
4. **No confirmed payoff links them** ([U2]). True, but silent — the missing payoff is missing on *both* readings and
   discriminates nothing.

### Verdict on (a)

**"Parallel Easter eggs sharing motifs" is REJECTED at the corpus's working standard.** The 2018 carvings and the 2025
trail read as **one continuous designed relay — sequential stages of one construction**. This is as settled as anything
upstream of the [K16] frontier gets: the links are [KNOWN]/primary-sourced, the seam is retro-validated, and four
independent design-level ties agree. The compound claim ("one *designed* puzzle") remains an authorship/design
**inference**, so it is minted as [SPECULATION]-grade ([S41], §5) — but U3 should stop treating it as an open fork.

## 3. (b) The mechanic question — open, and not U3's to decide

Whether the feather/boundary state system is an **interactive puzzle stage** ([H20]/[H22]/[H24] in their different
forms) or **epiphenomenal display machinery** on a read-and-navigate relay ([H27]; leaned into by the [#52] datamine's
zero-`spiderdream`-script-refs finding) is exactly the **[U29] frontier**, with Tests C/D as the discriminators. U3
adds nothing to that and should not duplicate it.

The point that *matters here*: **the relay verdict is invariant to (b)'s outcome.**

- Under [H27], the webs are waymarks *of the relay itself* — one puzzle trivially.
- Under [H24]/[H20]/[H22], they are an interactive stage *reached by* the relay — one puzzle with a mechanical layer.
- Even [H22]-R3 ("no combine") splits the web *groups from each other*, not the trail from the 2018 chain.

Separately, the **mechanical bridge** speculations ([S22]/[H21]: the oversized `B34` boundary reaching Butcher Creek +
Fort Brennand; [S35]: the Strange Man shack as the southern twin) would, if ever confirmed, extend "one puzzle" from
the narrative layer down to the **state layer**. They stay [SPECULATION] and are *not* load-bearing for §2's verdict.

## 5. The verdict — [S41]

> **[S41] (desk adjudication, 2026-07-04).** The 2018-era carving chain (Butcher Creek → Fort Brennand) and the
> 2025-decoded telegraph-pole web trail are **one continuous designed relay — sequential stages of one construction,
> not parallel eggs.** Anchors: the [K8] hand-off (primary wiki) **retro-validated** by what sits at the pointed-at
> pole ([K11]); shared signature medium + gating ([H13]/[K2]); both base-game ([K24]); a shared physical node
> (Oil Fields: [K10] + `B56`); the `spiderdream` asset name ↔ the [K10] dream carving. Design-intent inference →
> held as [SPECULATION], but adopted as the working frame; [U3]'s live residue is (b) the mechanic layer ([U29])
> and (c) satellite membership ([U6], matchsticks).

**Reopeners (what would reverse this):** a sourced dev statement splitting the two; a firsthand capture ([U10])
showing the Fort Brennand tower symbols don't plausibly depict a pole/factory; the Cornwall engraving or its
web-mapping reading failing verification; or a sourced alternate referent for the symbols that fits better than
Cornwall K&T.
