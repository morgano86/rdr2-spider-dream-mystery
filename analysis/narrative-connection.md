### Important correction (from research)
- The **final** native-arc battle, **"My Last Boy"** (Eagle Flies is mortally wounded), happens at the **Heartland Oil Fields**, *not* at the Cornwall Kerosene & Tar refinery. Cornwall **owns both** sites; wiki character pages blur them. This matters because our chain also passes through the Heartlands — another overlap node.

## The Eagle Flies / Rains Fall mission chain (for cross-reference)
Eastward Bound → The Gilded Cage → **Archaeology for Beginners** (Reservation) → **American Fathers I** (**Cornwall refinery**) → American Fathers II → The Fine Art of Conversation (Reservation) → **A Rage Unleashed** (Copperhead Landing/Beaver Hollow) → **The King's Son** (**Fort Wallace** rescue) → **My Last Boy** (**Heartland Oil Fields** + Reservation; Eagle Flies dies). *Theme:* the Army (Favours, out of Fort Wallace) provokes war to seize the Wapiti's **oil-rich** land (tied to Cornwall); Rains Fall's pacifism fails, Eagle Flies dies, the tribe is forced to **relocate to Canada**.

## Community theories that invoke the Native angle (documented SPECULATION)
- **Spider Grandmother** myth (Hopi/Navajo) — gave souls, separated peoples into tribes — proposed as the lens for the spider + feather symbolism. *(On the wiki's own speculation section.)*
- **Feathers = honor mechanic:** the **red vs black/white** feather split may map to **low vs high honor** — "no firm conclusions drawn."
- **Cut content:** the deflationary read — a leftover dream-sequence Easter egg (à la GTA V peyote) with no payoff — is at least as well-supported as the native-storyline theory.
- **No documented theory names Eagle Flies or Rains Fall specifically** as the solution; the native-connection theories operate at the level of **symbolism + shared locations**, not characters.

## Honest assessment
- **KNOWN:** the mystery is physically anchored in the **exact geography** of the army/oil-vs-Wapiti tragedy (Cornwall, Fort Wallace, Butcher Creek's industrial poisoning, the Wapiti NW), and uses **spider + feather** iconography that resonates with Native symbolism. 4 of 5 nodes sit inside or thematically adjacent to that conflict — unlikely to be pure chance.
- **SPECULATION/UNKNOWN:** no source establishes a **designed** link to the plot; the mystery has **no confirmed solution**; and **Fort Brennand** (Civil War / Lemoyne Raiders, no Wapiti tie) suggests the node-selection logic may be **geographic/atmospheric** (army forts + industrial sites in NW New Hanover) rather than "follow the Wapiti plot."
- **Use this framing:** "the mystery *shares the locations and iconography* of the army/oil-vs-Wapiti conflict" = **KNOWN**; "the mystery is *about* Eagle Flies/Rains Fall" = **plausible SPECULATION.**
