# Register Rock

> *New here? See the [README](../README.md) for the overview and the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the ID/tag conventions (`K13`, `[#89]`, `H27`…).*

A **memorial boulder inscribed with names and dates**, a point of interest in **The Heartlands, New Hanover** — between **Twin Stack Pass**, the **Heartland Oil Fields**, and **Emerald Ranch** (map: [`register-rock_map-location.webp`](../images/register-rock/register-rock_map-location.webp)). It mirrors the real-world Register Rock (Oregon Trail, Idaho) where emigrants carved their names.

> Why it may be a node: the investigator proposes the **third, debated symbol** in the Fort Brennand tower carving — the one read as a "telephone pole · factory · **oil puddle**" — may actually depict **this rock** (it's right in the area the symbols point to). If so, the **names carved on Register Rock could be the mystery's name-puzzle** ([H11]/[U24]) — directly relevant to the `LJ`/`SM` and matchstick (`EC`, `J+M`, `S+J`) letter questions. See [thread 02](../threads/02-butcher-creek-carvings.md) and [connections](../analysis/connections.md).

## [KNOWN]
- **It is a names-and-dates memorial rock** in central **The Heartlands** — the same area the Fort Brennand symbols steer toward (Heartlands / Oil Fields / Cornwall web cluster). In-world it prompts **"Inspect Rock."** Inspecting it makes the protagonist sketch the carvings into the journal (there are **two journal sketches** of the same face — wiki gallery `..._01/02/03/05` + `..._J.png`).
- **Disambiguation:** Register Rock is a **Point of Interest** (inspect → journal sketch), **not** one of the 10 *Rock Carvings* collectibles (the separate "Geology for Beginners" petroglyph set). Community search terms conflate the two.

## [UNKNOWN]
- **U24 — do the Register Rock names/initials encode anything, or match the mystery's letters?** Now that the **full** list is sourced, the initial cross-check against the mystery set `LJ` · `SM` · `EC` · `J+M` · `S+J` (multiset **{C,E,J,J,J,L,M,M,S,S}**) gives a **mixed** result — recorded here as a lead, **not** a solved match:
  - **🟢 J.M appears TWICE** — the bare mark **"Jm"** and the full name **"Jasper Munson"** — directly echoing the **`J+M`** matchstick pair at **Cornwall Kerosene & Tar** (the web-trail start, [K9]). This is the strongest new hit.
  - **🟢 S.G → "S. Gray"** → the **Gray** family → **Caliga Hall**, the **`S+J`** matchstick site ([K9]). (Known; still the cleanest narrative link.)
  - **🔴 No `LJ`, no `SM`, no `EC`** appears anywhere on the rock. Among the abundant carvings there is **no `L`-initial surname at all** and no `E…C` pair. So Register Rock covers part of the mystery's letter set (J, M, S, C are all present) but **does not** obviously supply `LJ`/`SM`/`EC`. A meaningful **partial-negative** — if the rock were the name-key, the absence of any `L` is awkward.
  - Other pairs present but **not** in the mystery set: `B.M`, `R.M`, `R.S`, `B.W`, `A.W`, `C.R`, `F.H`, `A.P`, `J.B`. → Next step is the scripted name-match (see [connections](../analysis/connections.md) open tasks); **keep all names live** per [S13].
- **Does the Fort Brennand "third symbol" actually depict this rock** (vs an oil puddle)? Unconfirmed — the symbol's reading is already debated ([thread 02](../threads/02-butcher-creek-carvings.md)). This is the key assumption for [H11]; the name-matching above only matters **if** the symbol really points here.

## [SPECULATION]
- **H11 — Register Rock IS the Fort Brennand third symbol, and its names are a puzzle.** If the carving points here rather than to "the oil fields generally," the **list of names** becomes a candidate **name/initial cipher** feeding `LJ`/`SM` and the matchstick pairs.
- **⚠️ Don't dismiss the "obvious reference" names ([S13], investigator's point).** The wiki's reading of some names as real-world or fictional-real-world references (e.g. A. West / B. Ward → *Batman*) is **speculation, not confirmed.** And if this is a genuine puzzle, the designers have every reason to choose names that **double as plausible outside references** — so players shrug them off as dev in-jokes and never test the in-game link. **A name doubling as a real-world reference is camouflage, not a reason to discard it.** Treat **all** names (the RDR legends Otis Miller / Billy Midnight, the "Batman" pair, and the rest) as **live candidates**; let the matching test decide, not the apparent in-joke.
