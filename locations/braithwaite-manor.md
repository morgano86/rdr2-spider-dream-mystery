# Braithwaite Manor

> *New here? See the [README](../README.md) for the overview and the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the ID/tag conventions (`K13`, `[#89]`, `H27`…).*

- **Region:** Scarlett Meadows, Lemoyne (S).
- **Type:** Plantation estate of the Braithwaite family (rivals of the Grays).

## Mystery role — [SPECULATION / "disconnected thread"]
- **Gertrude Braithwaite** is found (epilogue) locked in the property's **southern outhouse**, implied to have **starved**, **reciting a repeating number sequence** that **opens `1237645112`** (tail `…,1,2,10,3`). → thread [04](../threads/04-gertrude-numbers.md).
- ✅ **Her opening numbers are confirmed deliberate ([K23], 2026-06-13)** — the **Madam Nazar "Nazar Speaks"** machine in GTA Online speaks the same string. ⚠️ **Chronology:** the number is **RDR2-original (base game, Oct 2018)**; the GTA echo is a **later callback (Dec 2019)**, so it **corroborates intent**, it does *not* explain the number away. The **spider-trail link specifically is still undecided** (could be the trail, a standalone Braithwaite/Nazar egg, or undecipherable). A C-tier exposé separately alleges the Strange Man "Gertrude solved" *video* is a **hoax**. Cross-game detail → [gta-rdr2-crossover.md](../other-mysteries/gta-rdr2-crossover.md).
- Shares the **outhouse + numbers** motif with Butcher Creek / Fort Brennand — a real structural reason to keep it in play.
- Also a **cheat-carving site**: **"A SIMPLE LIFE, A BEAUTIFUL DEATH"** in a drawer in a small house here (see [cheat-codes.md](../analysis/cheat-codes.md)) — a death-themed phrase on the same property as Gertrude's grim death.

## Story role — [KNOWN]
- Central to the **Braithwaite–Gray feud** (Chapter 3): missions like "The Fine Joys of Tobacco" / "Blood Feuds, Ancient and Modern" (Jack kidnapped; the manor is burned). The Braithwaites are cruel Southern aristocracy.

## Why it matters
- The **structural rhyme** (outhouse + numbers) is still worth noting — but the once-"strongest" lead that Gertrude's numbers **index** the Butcher Creek/Fort Brennand tallies is **demoted** now that the numbers have a documented GTA/Nazar origin ([K23]). See [connections.md §2/§3](../analysis/connections.md).
