# Whiskey Tree (a.k.a. Bottle Tree)

- **Region:** central map, on a **high point on a hill** — its elevation makes it **visible from most of the web
  locations** (firsthand investigator data, 2026-06-14).
- **Type:** a lone tree hung with **whiskey bottles on ropes** (the source of the "whiskey tree" / "bottle tree" names);
  beside it, an **ever-smouldering fire pit**.
- **Coords:** not yet recorded.

## Mystery role — [SPECULATION] (the lone POI in the triple-boundary overlap)
- It is the **only POI inside the triple-boundary overlap zone** ([K28]) — the thin horizontal sliver where the Top, Middle,
  and Bottom respawn boundaries all coincide (a slim band of vertical height running the full width of the Middle/Connector
  boundary's centre). The overlap is the one place you can be inside **>1 boundary at once**, the candidate mechanism for
  *holding* a colour-group's shot-off state ([H20]) — though the one direct test of that idea (camp here, then leave the
  boundary) **reset** the feather (see [K29]/§5a; negative-leaning).
- **The red-hue fire-pit motif ([S23]):** the whiskey tree's **ever-smouldering fire pit glows the same unusual red hue** as
  the **Butcher Creek pentagram** ([K5]) and the **Fort Brennand** fire pit. Such pits in *exactly* this red are unusual in
  RDR2. The shared hue is investigator data; **any tie to the spider mystery is unconfirmed [SPECULATION]** (colour-matching
  by eye is error-prone; RDR2 reuses fire VFX; no named asset or pointer links the tree to a mystery node).

- **No confirmed connection** to the spider mystery. It earned a dossier on **two coincidences**: (i) ~~its position in the
  **sole triple-overlap zone**~~ and (ii) the **anomalous red fire pit** shared with two confirmed mystery sites.

  the Connector spine touches both. The tree therefore sits inside **one** boundary like any other spot on the spine —
  an unremarkable position, not a unique one. **Only leg (ii), the fire-pit hue, still stands**, and it is an eyeball
  match ([S23]). ⟹ **This dossier now rests on a single unsourced visual coincidence.** Keep it — the hue question is

  [H22]-R1 / [H24] / [H25] protocols is **gone** (those protocols are updated).
