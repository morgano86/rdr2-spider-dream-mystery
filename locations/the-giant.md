# The Giant

- **Region:** Cave **east of Fairvale Shanty, Grizzlies East, Ambarino** (mountainside east of the Calumet River).
- **Type:** Hidden NPC "stranger" encounter. Voiced by Jake Hart; likeness based on actor **Rondo Hatton** (acromegaly).

## Mystery role — [SPECULATION]
- The endpoint of the **bird-carving lead** (thread [06](../threads/06-bird-carving-giant-wapiti.md)): the **flock that guides you to the Giant spawns at Calumet Ravine**, the direction the Fort Wallace bird carving may indicate. Strange Man's theory ties the carving to this flock. **Unproven**; possibly cut content.

## Facts — [KNOWN]
- **You hear but never see him** — he speaks from behind a **sealed cave entrance**; an invisible NPC voices him (and can be killed).
- **Prerequisite: study ≥30 species** (Compendium "Studied"), **excluding fish and horses** — gates the encounter and the guiding flock.
- **Loves animals** — the bird flock leads to him and he says he's good with animals. Lonely, gentle; sample lines: *"Hello there, friend. How are you?"*, *"I'm rather lonely, you see... I'm too big."*
- **Cut content:** originally meant to be **seen** (1899 & 1907 models exist); cut dialogue: recently escaped a **circus**; a second unused cave nearby.

## Why it matters
- Thematically rich (animals, feathers, a hidden being you can't see — "dream"-like) and **gated by an action (30 animals)**, which is the kind of **prerequisite** the mystery might expect. But the cut-content evidence is strong. Evaluate, don't assume it's a mystery step.
