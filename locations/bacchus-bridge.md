# Bacchus Bridge

> *New here? See the [README](../README.md) for the overview and the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the ID/tag conventions (`K13`, `[#89]`, `H27`…).*

A tall wooden **truss rail bridge** spanning a gorge of the **Dakota River**, **Cumberland Forest, New Hanover**, near the Ambarino mountains. Lore-billed the *"Gateway to the West,"* it carries the rail line connecting Big Valley / West Elizabeth to Annesburg. Overview image: [`bacchus-bridge_overview.webp`](../images/bacchus-bridge/bacchus-bridge_overview.webp).

> Why it's a node: it carries a **hidden, empty heart carving** ([K22]) that is the **blank twin of the Flatneck Station "Lillie ♥ Alfred" heart**, and the investigator reports it sits **in line of sight of the Fort Wallace bird carving** ([K16]) on the NW frontier. See [thread 06](../threads/06-bird-carving-giant-wapiti.md).

## [KNOWN]
- **The empty heart carving — [K22] (investigator data, 2026-06-13).** Carved into a **leg/support of the bridge** is a **cupid-style heart with an arrow through it**, **reasonably difficult to find**. It is **identical in shape** to the heart in the Flatneck Station *"Lillie ♥ Alfred"* tree carving — but **empty**: **no message, no names, no initials**, just the heart-and-arrow. *(The filled Flatneck version, recovered from the datamined `…treeplaceholder2` texture, is held for comparison: [`flatneck-station_heart-carving_datamine.png`](../images/bacchus-bridge/flatneck-station_heart-carving_datamine.png) — "MARCH 1898 · LILLIE ♥ ALFRED · ALWAYS IN MY HEART …·  FEBRUARY 1903".)*
- **Location/role.** Cumberland Forest rail bridge over the Dakota River gorge (the same region as **Fort Wallace**, which bears the bird/"w" carving) — so a line of sight between the two is geographically plausible.

> **Same pocket — a third feature (separate egg, 2026-06-21):** one of the **"Geology for Beginners" rock carvings** (a **winged figure**) sits on a **Dakota River cliff NE of Fort Wallace**, between here and Cotorra Springs ([K33]). It's part of the **Francis Sinclair** time-traveller egg — **NOT verified-linked to the spider mystery** ([thread 08](../threads/08-francis-sinclair-mural.md), watch-item [S26]) — but worth knowing this gorge holds three distinct carved/anomalous things ([K16] birds, [K22] heart, [K33] winged figure) within a short ride.

## [SPECULATION]
- **H10 — the two hearts are a deliberate paired motif.** Flatneck = the *filled* heart (a named romantic carving, itself a separate well-known easter egg); Bacchus = the *empty* heart, **hidden** and **aligned with the bird carving**. If the emptiness is intentional ("the names are missing — go find/fill them"), it could fold into the mystery's **name questions** (`LJ`/`SM`, the matchstick pairs, and the **Register Rock** names — [H11]/[U24]). Strictly a hypothesis; sourcing is firsthand + one separate-egg datamine.
- **Date coincidence, noted not weighted:** the Flatneck heart reads **1898 / 1903**; the Bacchus Bridge **train disaster** is dated *"prior to 1898."* Probably unrelated, logged so it isn't "rediscovered."

## Lore (in-world)
- Site of the **Bacchus Bridge train disaster** (before 1898): a group of **U.S. Army deserters faked their deaths** by derailing a train, killing **at least a dozen** other soldiers. *(Source: Red Dead Wiki — Bacchus Bridge.)*
