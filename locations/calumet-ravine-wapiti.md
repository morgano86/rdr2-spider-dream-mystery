# Calumet Ravine & the Wapiti Indian Reservation

- **Region:** Grizzlies East, Ambarino (NW). Calumet Ravine lies **east of the Wapiti Indian Reservation**.
- **Type:** Wilderness ravine (source of the Dakota River over Donner Falls) + the Wapiti homeland.

## Mystery role — [SPECULATION / cut-content lead]
- The trail's `NW` heading and the Fort Wallace **bird carving** point toward this sparse corner.
- **Calumet Ravine is where the flock that leads to the Giant spawns** (west side of the Calumet River), flying east to the Giant's cave. → thread [06](../threads/06-bird-carving-giant-wapiti.md).
- NW of the guitar pole points to a **beta location** (believed Wapiti / an army camp / Iron Cloud) — a strong **cut-content** signal. The "empty" feel is partly because it's edge-of-map frontier where the Wapiti were forced to relocate.

## POIs — [KNOWN]
- **Ancient Tomahawk** (in a tree target, NE side); a **bone collectible** (SE, hill east of Donner Falls); a shack with a **Witches Cauldron** far east (Hair Tonic + Kentucky Bourbon).

## Story role — [KNOWN]
- The **home turf of the Rains Fall / Eagle Flies arc**; mission **"Archaeology for Beginners"** rides through the Reservation (a desecrated shrine). The arc ends with the Wapiti forced to relocate to Canada.

## Why it matters
- The mystery's **apparent destination** and the **symbolic heart** of the Native reading (feathers, `wap_` asset prefix, Spider Grandmother). But also the **most likely place the trail is simply cut content.** Hold both possibilities.
