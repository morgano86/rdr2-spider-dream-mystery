# Fort Wallace

- **Region:** Cumberland Forest, New Hanover (toward the Grizzlies).
- **Type:** US Army fort, commanded by **Colonel Henry Favours**.

## Mystery role — [KNOWN]
- The trail's `NW` + guitar heading points here; the fort contains **two guitars** — one points toward a tower; the **second, on the tower, points to its roof**.
- In the **westernmost tower**, above one guitar, are **two "w"/bird symbols modelled into the geometry** (same hidden-mesh technique as Butcher Creek). Disputed meaning — see thread [06](../threads/06-bird-carving-giant-wapiti.md) and the [carving test](../analysis/carving-technique.md).
- ⚠️ **Updated 2026-06-13:** Fort Wallace is a **waypoint, not the dead-end.** The trail **continues past it** — the current (unverified, post-Jan-2026) frontier runs NW toward **Calumet Ravine / the Giant's birds** and a **"?" carving on an out-of-bounds mountain**. **No secondary source corroborates the wiki-only "Spider Gorge" reading** ([U13](../findings/unknowns.md)).
- Images: [`../images/fort-wallace/`](../images/fort-wallace/) (bird symbols, "?" pareidolia shots).
- **🆕 File-data aside — the night-lighting ladder ([K50]/[S47]/[U39], [#89], 2026-09-01). Data, not a lead.** A game-wide census of time-gated props found **four Fort Wallace buildings each on their own hand-authored schedule**, with staggered starts **21 → 22 → 23 → 00**: `old_01_bsmith_em` (blacksmith, @ 339.20, 1480.63, 180.27) **21:00–24:00** · `old_01_quater2_em` (@ 318.46, 1486.93, 181.96) **22:00–03:00** · `old_01_quater_em` (@ 316.52, 1499.54, 181.96) **23:00–05:00** · `old_01_capt_em` (captain, @ 344.38, 1509.17, 183.02) **00:00–06:00**. The dev region code is **`old_` = "Old Fort Wallace"** (confirmed via the `AIMEMLOC_CML_OldFortWallace` / `establisher_old_fort_wallace_1` strings) — useful for any future file search here. Context: **921 of the game's 994 time-gated props are on one standard 21:00–07:00 schedule**, and four deviations in one settlement occurs nowhere else — but the favoured reading is **set dressing** (a fort settling down watch by watch), and this must **not** be treated as a step past [K16].

## Story role — [KNOWN]
- Col. Favours deliberately **provokes the Wapiti** to justify seizing their (oil-rich) land.
- **Eagle Flies is captured and imprisoned here** after the Wapiti/gang are routed by the fort's cannon.
- **Mission "The King's Son":** Arthur + **Charles Smith** infiltrate at night, **free Eagle Flies**, blow the wall, escape by canoe. (Also where Arthur tells Charles about his TB.)

## Why it matters
- A **strong narrative node** — *the* army fort of the Wapiti arc — and the trail's current **waypoint** (not its end). The bird symbols here are the bridge to the NW/Giant lead.
