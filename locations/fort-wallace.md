# Fort Wallace

- **Region:** Cumberland Forest, New Hanover (toward the Grizzlies).
- **Type:** US Army fort, commanded by **Colonel Henry Favours**.

## Mystery role — [KNOWN]
- The trail's `NW` + guitar heading points here; the fort contains **two guitars** — one points toward a tower; the **second,
  on the tower, points to its roof**.
- In the **westernmost tower**, above one guitar, are **two "w"/bird symbols modelled into the geometry** (same hidden-mesh
  technique as Butcher Creek). Disputed meaning — see thread [06](../threads/06-bird-carving-giant-wapiti.md) and the
  [carving test](../analysis/carving-technique.md).
- ⚠️ **Updated 2026-06-13:** Fort Wallace is a **waypoint, not the dead-end.** The trail **continues past it** — the current
  (unverified, post-Jan-2026) frontier runs NW toward **Calumet Ravine / the Giant's birds** and a **"?" carving on an
  out-of-bounds mountain**. **No secondary source corroborates the wiki-only "Spider Gorge" reading** ([U13](../findings/unknowns.md)).
- Images: [`../images/fort-wallace/`](../images/fort-wallace/) (bird symbols, "?" pareidolia shots).

## Story role — [KNOWN]
- Col. Favours deliberately **provokes the Wapiti** to justify seizing their (oil-rich) land.
- **Eagle Flies is captured and imprisoned here** after the Wapiti/gang are routed by the fort's cannon.
- **Mission "The King's Son":** Arthur + **Charles Smith** infiltrate at night, **free Eagle Flies**, blow the wall, escape
  by canoe. (Also where Arthur tells Charles about his TB.)

## Why it matters
- A **strong narrative node** — *the* army fort of the Wapiti arc — and the trail's current **waypoint** (not its end). The bird symbols here
  are the bridge to the NW/Giant lead.
