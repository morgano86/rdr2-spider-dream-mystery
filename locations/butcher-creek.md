# Butcher Creek

- **Region:** Roanoke Ridge, New Hanover (NE), near the Murfree Brood territory.
- **Type:** Poor, isolated, inbred-coded settlement; setting of the "Wisdom of the Elders" stranger mission.

## Mystery role — [KNOWN] (the START of the whole chain)
- A **glowing red pentagram** appears under one of the houses **~4–5 AM**.
- **Five outhouses**, each with a **tally mark 1–5**; connecting them in order forms a **second pentagram**.
- **Outhouse #4** bears the carvings **`LJ`**, **`SM`**, and a **Fort Brennand symbol** (→ next node).
- Full detail + image in thread [02](../threads/02-butcher-creek-carvings.md) and
  [`../images/butcher-creek/`](../images/butcher-creek/).

## Story role — [KNOWN]
- **"The Wisdom of the Elders":** the town's "curse" is actually **lead/arsenic poisoning** from an abandoned mine in the
  water (Elysian Pool). A **fraudulent "shaman," sent by the Roanoke Fuel Company**, exploits the villagers to get them to
  **sign away the company's liability**. Arthur exposes him; the villagers still believe in the curse.

## Why it matters
- The literal **entry point** of the mystery, with an explicit **occult framing** (pentagrams) layered over a
  **fake-mysticism-hides-industrial-poisoning** story — the **same thematic register** as the Wapiti arc (industry +
  dispossession + coerced paperwork), though the victims here are poor whites. Thematic, not character-level, link.
