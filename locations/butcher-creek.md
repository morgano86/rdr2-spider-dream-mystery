# Butcher Creek

> *New here? See the [README](../README.md) for the overview and the [glossary](../GLOSSARY.md) for the ID/tag conventions (`K13`, `[#89]`, `H27`…).*

- **Region:** Roanoke Ridge, New Hanover (NE), near the Murfree Brood territory.
- **Type:** Poor, isolated, inbred-coded settlement; setting of the "Wisdom of the Elders" stranger mission.

## Mystery role — [KNOWN] (the START of the whole chain)
- A **glowing red pentagram** appears under one of the houses **~4–5 AM**. **⚡ Pinned in the game files 2026-09-01 ([K52], [#89]):** it is **`cablemesh277747_hvlit001` @ (2592.52, 831.74, 82.79)**, 1–3 m from `but_house_hd002`, **active hour `04:00`** — the **same drawable family as the spider-web strands** ([S41] anchor), and the **only single-hour prop in the whole game allowed to change on screen** (`0x1000010`), which file-confirms the firsthand [K30] observation that this pentagram appears/disappears while watched but the webs do not.
- **Five outhouses**, each with a **tally mark 1–5**; connecting them in order forms a **second pentagram**.
- **Outhouse #4** bears the carvings **`LJ`**, **`SM`**, and a **Fort Brennand symbol** (→ next node). The carved `LJ`/`SM` are **directly part of the mystery** ([KNOWN] via the carving technique [K15] + co-location; only their *meaning* is open, [U4]).
- **Oddities clustering on outhouse #4** (the same stall): the chain **branches from tally 4, not tally 5** — one short of the 1–5 sequence's end ([U27]); and there's a **3-in-a-row line of droppings on its roof** (two large + a gap + one small at the doorframe edge — [U28]; [`../images/butcher-creek/butcher-creek_outhouse4-roof-droppings.jpg`](../images/butcher-creek/butcher-creek_outhouse4-roof-droppings.jpg)). - Full detail + image in thread [02](../threads/02-butcher-creek-carvings.md) and [`../images/butcher-creek/`](../images/butcher-creek/).
- **Inside the top respawn boundary ([K28], investigator 2026-06-14):** Butcher Creek is enveloped by the oversized top (B34/Cornwall) boundary — alongside **Fort Brennand** and Valentine — so the web shot-state ([K21]/[K29]) can be carried here without resetting (the basis for the state-carry reading [H21]). ⚠️ **Deflated 2026-09-01 ([K48]/[H28]):** that "boundary" is the extent box of an ordinary region ymap (`jklm_7_10_rds_props_strm_0`), so its reach is not a design choice — and the pentagram's coordinates sit within **~1.1 m of that box's own X-max corner (2593.6, 880.6)**, which probably means the boundary reaches Butcher Creek **because the pentagram is one of the entities it is drawn around** ([U36] settles this). - **The pentagram's red glow hue ([S23]):** the floorboard pentagram's **specific red hue** is **shared** by ever-smouldering fire pits at **Fort Brennand** and the **[whiskey/bottle tree](whiskey-tree.md)** — an unusual-red visual motif recurring at mystery sites. The match is investigator data; the *tie* is **[SPECULATION]**.

## Story role — [KNOWN]
- **"The Wisdom of the Elders":** the town's "curse" is actually **lead/arsenic poisoning** from an abandoned mine in the water (Elysian Pool). A **fraudulent "shaman," sent by the Roanoke Fuel Company**, exploits the villagers to get them to **sign away the company's liability**. Arthur exposes him; the villagers still believe in the curse.

## Why it matters
- The literal **entry point** of the mystery, with an explicit **occult framing** (pentagrams) layered over a **fake-mysticism-hides-industrial-poisoning** story — the **same thematic register** as the Wapiti arc (industry + dispossession + coerced paperwork), though the victims here are poor whites. Thematic, not character-level, link.
