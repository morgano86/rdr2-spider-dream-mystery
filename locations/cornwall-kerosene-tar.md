# Cornwall Kerosene & Tar

- **Region:** New Hanover, near **Annesburg** (Roanoke Ridge area, NE).
- **Type:** Oil refinery owned by **Leviticus Cornwall** — the gang's chief industrialist antagonist.

## Mystery role — [KNOWN]
- The **start of the spider-web trail.** Fort Brennand's three symbols (telephone pole, factory, oil puddle) direct the
  player **here**, to a **telephone pole bearing the spider engraving** — the index/map glyph for the 8 webs.
- In the web catalogue this is web **Cornwall / `spiderdream03` / B34** (black feather, 3–4 AM). See
  [WEBS-MANIFEST](../images/webs/WEBS-MANIFEST.md).
- ⭐ **Also holds the `J + M` matchstick set** (investigator data, 2026-06-13) — i.e. a **letter clue co-located with the
  web-trail start**, strong evidence the matchsticks belong to the puzzle. → thread [03](../threads/03-matchstick-letters.md).
- Image: [`../images/webs/web_cornwall_b34_engraving.webp`](../images/webs/web_cornwall_b34_engraving.webp).

## Story role — [KNOWN]
- Mission **"American Fathers I":** Arthur + **Eagle Flies** infiltrate the refinery to steal army documents proving the US
  Army intends to seize Wapiti land for its **oil**; Eagle Flies sets off a **diversion blast** to cover the escape. → direct
  link between this site and the Wapiti storyline.
- Also used in **"Pouring Forth Oil II"** (steal an oil wagon for a train robbery — gang business, not the native arc).

## Why it matters
- One of the **strongest narrative nodes**: it ties the mystery's geography to **Cornwall** (oil/industry villain) and to the
  Wapiti's grievance. See [narrative-connection.md](../analysis/narrative-connection.md).
- **Note:** the *final* native-arc battle ("My Last Boy") is at the **Heartland Oil Fields**, not here — Cornwall owns both;
  wiki pages blur them. Both sit on our map of nodes.
