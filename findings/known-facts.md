# Known facts (cross-thread)

Facts that are verifiable in-game and/or corroborated across multiple reliable sources. Each links to its thread. If a fact
later proves shaky, demote it to [unknowns](unknowns.md) with a note.

## The mystery itself
- **K1.** A deliberate, time-gated, multi-location Easter egg known as the **Spider Dream / Spider Web Mystery** exists in
  RDR2. → [01](../threads/01-spider-dream.md)
- **K2.** It went largely undocumented for ~7 years (game released **Oct 2018**) and resurfaced in **late Dec 2025**. → [01](../threads/01-spider-dream.md)
- **K3.** A **former Rockstar QA tester, Adam Butterworth** (now an audio test engineer at Remedy), publicly reacted to the
  viral mystery on **5 Jan 2026**: *"Absolutely wild people have found this. I remember hearing about this and thinking it
  would never be discovered."* This **corroborates that the Easter egg is real and deliberate** — but **not** that he designed
  or worked on it ("hearing about this" = secondhand awareness). ⚠️ **Two-sided correction:** our *first* pass over-claimed
  "Butterworth confirmed design intent" (refuted); a *later* pass over-corrected by stripping his name as "unsourced." The
  accurate position: the **name + quote are now widely reported** (Hypebeast, GameRant, RockstarINTEL, Dexerto, Popverse,
  RDR2.org, +others — all tracing to **one X post**, so **B/C-tier, not A**: a single self-identified account, unverified via
  credits/LinkedIn), and the quote confirms **reality, not authorship**. Outlets that say it "proves Rockstar placed it with
  intent" are stating **journalist inference**, not his words. *(Verified deep-research pass, 2026-06-13, 3-0.)*
  → [01](../threads/01-spider-dream.md), [U8](unknowns.md)

## Butcher Creek (Roanoke Ridge)

- **K5.** Connecting the five outhouses **in numerical order forms a pentagram**, mirroring a **glowing red floorboard
  pentagram** visible **~4–5 AM** under one of the houses. → [02](../threads/02-butcher-creek-carvings.md)
- **K6.** **Outhouse #4** bears extra carvings: **`LJ`**, **`SM`**, and a **Fort Brennand symbol** (the user's "LJ SM on the
  back of toilet 4"). → [02](../threads/02-butcher-creek-carvings.md)
- **K7.** **Fort Brennand** contains more **tally marks** (**6** in the outhouse, **7** at a tower entrance) and **three
  tower symbols**: a **telephone pole**, a **factory**, and an **oil puddle**. *(Primary wiki.)* → [02](../threads/02-butcher-creek-carvings.md)
- **K8.** Those symbols direct the player to a **telephone pole near Cornwall Kerosene & Tar** bearing the **spider
  engraving** — the start of the web trail. *(Primary wiki; corrects the earlier "points to the oil-fields refinery"
  reading.)* → [05](../threads/05-spider-web-trail-2025.md)

## The Spider Web trail (Roanoke → Grizzlies)
- **K11.** The spider engraving maps **7 more webs** (each with a feather), each **time-locked to a different night hour**
  (2–3am, 3–4am, …); one web is near **Saint Denis**. A **central** set of webs (1–2am, **no feathers**) lines up to spell
  **`N`** + a telephone pole. *(Primary wiki.)* → [05](../threads/05-spider-web-trail-2025.md)
- **K12.** Going **north** and shooting the indicated pole reveals **`W` + five telephone-pole glyphs** (= travel **five poles
  west**; our `W ✞✞✞✞✞` shorthand is accurate — the "crosses" are **telephone-pole symbols**, per rdr2.org). Five poles west,
  another pole (shot repeatedly) reveals **`NW`** + a symbol **believed to be a guitar** (consistently hedged — not confirmed).
  **`NW` points to Fort Wallace**, which holds **two guitars** (one pointing toward a tower; the second, on the tower, pointing
  to its roof). ⚠️ **Updated 2026-06-13:** Fort Wallace is a **waypoint, not the terminus** — the trail continues past it (the
  current cold frontier is further NW, [06](../threads/06-bird-carving-giant-wapiti.md)). **No secondary source ties the guitar
  marker to Spider Gorge** — that is wiki-only speculation ([U13](unknowns.md)). *(Verified pass, 2026-06-13, 3-0.)*
  → [05](../threads/05-spider-web-trail-2025.md)
- **K13.** There are **5 black + 3 red feathers** total; the feather files are named **"spiderdream"** (texture
  `wap_gen_feather01`). *(Primary wiki, file-data.)* → [05](../threads/05-spider-web-trail-2025.md)
- **K13a.** **The 8 webs are catalogued** (community research site + file names `spiderdream01–08`): **5 black** = Cornwall(B34),
  Oil Fields(B56), Overflow(B23), Emerald(B45), Ringneck(BL56); **3 red** = Saint Denis(R34), Southfield(R45), Scarlett(R23);
  code = colour+hour. Centre cluster = 1–2 AM, no feathers, spells `N`+pole. Full table → [WEBS-MANIFEST](../images/webs/WEBS-MANIFEST.md).
- **K13b.** A **non-respawn shooting chain** is documented: `B23 → B45 → B56 → BL56` (B56/BL56 interchangeable) — implying a
  correct **order** exists. **Feather position/orientation within each web is undocumented online** (capture in-game). → [05](../threads/05-spider-web-trail-2025.md)
  *(The boundary mechanic K21 explains **why** this chain doesn't respawn: these four are the "Connector" boundary — see [H9].)*
- **K21.** Feather respawn is **boundary-gated**. When you shoot/hit a feather it **falls to the ground and cannot be picked up
  or interacted with**; crucially, it **stays down — does not respawn in the web — for as long as you remain inside the
  boundary that web is tied to**, and **respawns once you leave** that boundary. This is a deliberate mechanic. There are
  **three boundaries**: one in the **north**, one in the **south**, and a **connector** that links the north boundary to the
  south. Multiple webs can share a boundary. *(Firsthand investigator data, 2026-06-13 — recorded high-trust per CLAUDE.md;
  the per-web assignment is hypothesis [H9], the exact membership is open question [U22].)* → [WEBS-MANIFEST](../images/webs/WEBS-MANIFEST.md)

## Carving technique (how we separate clue from pareidolia)
- **K15.** The signature symbols (`LJ`/`SM`, Fort Brennand icons, Fort Wallace birds) are **modelled into the mesh geometry and
  disguised under the moss/wood texture** — *"made from the geometry … shaded with the moss texture … not seen anywhere else"*
  except Butcher Creek. The webs + pentagram are **mesh "cables"** (cf. GTA V Mt. Chiliad, also 1–2 AM). **Named assets**
  (`spiderdream`, `wap_gen_feather01`) prove authorship. → [carving-technique.md](../analysis/carving-technique.md)

## How the mystery presents in-game
- **K20.** The **Spider Dream mystery has NO in-game tracking of any kind**: no mission/quest-log entry, no notification, no
  on-screen "webs found" counter. It is **entirely finding-driven and invisible to the player** — you only know it exists by
  noticing the carvings/webs yourself. It *may* be tracked silently in the background (unknown). *(Investigator data,
  2026-06-13; consistent with every source, none of which describes an in-game tracker.)* ⚠️ **This corrects an earlier draft**
  that mistakenly said the spider mystery "does not properly disappear from the log" — that lingering **log entry belongs to
  the *dreamcatchers* side mission**, which the spider wiki cites only by analogy. → [01](../threads/01-spider-dream.md),
  [dreamcatchers.md](../analysis/dreamcatchers.md), [U17](unknowns.md)

## Gertrude Braithwaite (Scarlett Meadows)
- **K14.** Gertrude is found locked in the Braithwaite **outhouse** (epilogue), implied to have **starved**, **reciting a
  repeating number sequence.** → [04](../threads/04-gertrude-numbers.md)
- **K23.** Gertrude's recitation **opens with `1 2 3 7 6 4 5 1 1 2`** (`123 7645112`), and this exact string is a **deliberate
  Rockstar cross-game Easter egg**: the **Madam Nazar "Nazar Speaks" fortune-teller machine** in **GTA Online** speaks the
  *same* number. *(B-tier, multi-outlet: GamesRadar, PCGamesN, TheGamer, SVG — sources #24/#40/#41/#42.)* ⚠️ **Chronology
  (corrected 2026-06-13, user-flagged):** the number is **RDR2-original — Gertrude is base-game content (Oct 26, 2018)**; the
  GTA Online Nazar machine that echoes it arrived **later**, with the **Diamond Casino Heist update (Dec 12, 2019)**, ~14
  months after. So the GTA side is a **later callback, NOT a "prior origin"** — it does **not** explain the RDR2 numbers away.
  **Implication (revised):** that Rockstar deliberately echoed the exact digits in another game is evidence the number is
  **intentional and significant**, not throwaway. It does **not**, by itself, prove the numbers belong to the *spider* mystery
  specifically — that link is **still open** ([U6]); they may key the spider trail, a standalone Braithwaite/Nazar puzzle, or
  nothing decipherable. A separate community exposé (C-tier, #43) alleges the "Gertrude solved → spider web" *video* is a
  **hoax** (independent of the chronology point). Full crossover analysis → [gta-rdr2-crossover.md](../analysis/gta-rdr2-crossover.md).
  Ties [U6](unknowns.md), [U18](unknowns.md), [U19](unknowns.md). → [04](../threads/04-gertrude-numbers.md)
- **K24.** **GTA V's two Mount Chiliad spider-webs are base-game, not DLC.** GTA V (2013 launch) contains **two spider-webs on
  Mt Chiliad** that appear **only 1–2 AM**, one **beneath the cable-car station** housing the Chiliad mural; they have existed
  **since the 2013 launch and were never added by an update** (Rockstar patched/removed other elements over a decade; these
  remained). They use the **same cable shader** as RDR2's webs ([K15]); RDR2's centre webs are **also 1–2 AM** ([K11]). The
  RDR2↔GTA V web link was popularized **~Jan 2026 (Oddheader)**. **So the webs in *both* games are original-to-launch — neither
  came from DLC** (only the 2019 Nazar number callback did). *(B-tier: Dexerto #45.)* Bears on [U18](unknowns.md). →
  [gta-rdr2-crossover.md](../analysis/gta-rdr2-crossover.md), [05](../threads/05-spider-web-trail-2025.md)
